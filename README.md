### LAB 12 – AI Presentation Lab

In Lab 12, I learned how to build and evaluate an AI-assisted aviation management presentation on **Improving Passenger Experience at Airports Using Generative AI**. The presentation covered passenger bottlenecks, Generative AI applications, operational impact, and implementation and governance.

During the audit, several problems were identified. One slide contained an unsupported claim that Generative AI had reduced airport delays by 85% globally. Another slide contained too much text, the presentation had inconsistent colours, and one visual was unrelated to airport passenger operations.

The problems were classified using a Severity-and-Risk Matrix. Unsupported statistics were treated as a critical issue because they could create reputational and compliance risks. The presentation was then revised using verified or clearly marked information, cleaner visuals, consistent branding, and shorter bullet points.

This lab taught me that AI-generated presentations must be reviewed carefully for factual accuracy, logical structure, readability, visual consistency, and business risk before being used professionally.
